NestPrep helps one household run its week: school lunches planned per child, the family calendar, the grocery list, to-dos and chores, the papers you keep needing, and what a nanny or helper needs for a shift. To do that it has to hold information about you and the people you live with, including your children. This policy says what we hold, why, where it is kept, who else touches it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it.

We wrote it to be read. Where the law needs a precise word we use it, and where a plain sentence does the job we use that instead.

## 2. Who this policy is about

This policy covers everybody whose information is in NestPrep:

- **Account holders**, the adults who sign in with their own account.
- **Household members without an account**, such as a child or grandparent whose profile an adult added.
- **Children using a kid device**, a tablet or phone a parent has signed in as their child.
- **Helpers, nannies, babysitters and carers** a household invites, who sign in with their own account and see only what the household allows.
- **People named by a household**, such as an emergency contact or a person allowed to collect a child from school.
- **Visitors to our website**, including anyone who asks us to delete an account from it.

## 3. What we collect, and why

We only collect what a part of the app needs to work. Most of it is information you, or another adult in your household, type or choose in the app.

### Your account

- Your name, your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your Google profile picture.
- How you sign in: with Google, or with an email address and a password. Passwords are handled by Google's Firebase Authentication service. We never see or store your password.
- A random account identifier, the households you belong to, which one you last had open, and when you created your account and last signed in.
- Which version of this policy and of our Terms you accepted, and when.

We use this to sign you in, to show you your households, and to keep a record that you agreed to these terms.

### Your household and its members

- The household's name and time zone.
- A profile for each member: a display name, a colour, a role (admin, parent, kid, helper or carer), and, if the household chooses, a birthday.
- For each helper, carer and kid, what they are allowed to see and change, as the household's parents set it.
- Invite codes, which are single use and expire, and which member profile an account claimed.

We use this so the people in a household see the same week, and so each person sees only what the household's parents decided they should.

### Family profiles, including children's information

For each member, and most often for each child, a household may record likes, dislikes, **allergies and food rules** (for example nut-free), **medication**, school, grade and clothing and shoe sizes. Children's profiles are the heart of lunch planning: the app uses allergies and food rules to stop a lunch that would break them from being planned.

Allergies and medication are **health information**, which POPIA treats as special personal information. Information about a child is also specially protected. See section 5 for how we handle both.

### Kid devices

A parent can sign a child's device in as that child's profile with a short code that lasts ten minutes and can be used once. A kid device has no email address, no name of its own and no password. For each kid device we store a random identifier, which household and child profile it belongs to, the label the parent typed for it (for example "Mia's tablet"), and when it was paired and by whom. A parent can sign a kid device out at any time.

### Avatars

Each member can be shown by a picture instead of their initial. An avatar can be one of NestPrep's own characters, a photo, or a picture made by our AI (see AI avatar creations below). Whichever it is, everybody in the household sees it, and so do the household's NestPrep Home frames.

- **The avatar each member chose**, the accessories they wear, and the ones a child has bought with stars. A child's character grows as they earn stars, worked out from the star history the household already keeps.
- **A photo avatar** is compressed on your phone, and its location and camera details are removed, before it is uploaded. A photo avatar is **never sent to any AI**. We keep the photo until it is changed or removed, and then delete it.
- **AI avatar creations**, kept beside the drawing, photo or words they were made from, until the family deletes them.

A family member (an admin or a parent) can change anybody's avatar, and every member can change their own, including a child on a kid device.

### Lunches, meals, groceries, to-dos and chores

- Lunch box plans per child, the household's library of lunch items and the allergens each contains, favourites, the prep list, and what came home uneaten.
- The weekly meal plan and the household's meal library.
- Recipes your household adds: what you typed or saved, any pictures, who added each one and, for a recipe saved from a website, the page's address and the site's name. Only your household can see them.
- When you save a recipe from a link, your phone asks that website for the page and its pictures, just as it does when you open the link, so the website sees your phone's internet address. We keep only the recipe you confirm and the pictures you choose.
- When you add recipes from a **PDF**, whether you choose it in NestPrep or share it into NestPrep from another app, the PDF stays on your phone. Pictures of its pages are sent to be read, and are not kept; the PDF itself is never uploaded or stored, and the app lets go of it as soon as the recipes are read. A PDF you share in is read only after you say so. We keep only the recipes you choose to add, with a picture from the PDF where it has one, and the PDF's name.
- The grocery list: items, who added them and who ticked them off.
- To-dos, routines, who they are assigned to and when they were done.
- Chores that earn stars, the rewards a parent sets, and each child's star balance and history, including the stars spent on avatar accessories.

### The calendar

- Events you and your household add: titles, times, who they are for, how they repeat, and notes.
- If you connect a **Google or Outlook calendar**, we keep the permission those services give us to read your events (an access credential) on our servers only, never on other household members' phones, and copy the events into your household calendar. We only ask to read, never to change, your calendars. You can disconnect at any time.
- NestPrep's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. We read your Google Calendar events only to show them in your household's calendar, its morning digest and on its nestprep Home frame. We never use them for advertising, never sell them or give them to advertisers or data brokers, never use them to train AI models, and nobody at NestPrep reads them unless you ask us to for support, or the law or security requires it.
- From a connected Google or Outlook calendar we keep only two things: the account's email address, so you can see which account is connected, and, for each event on its main calendar from a week ago to 120 days ahead, the event's title and its start and end time. We skip events that are cancelled or that you declined, and we never keep descriptions, locations, attendee lists or your other calendars.
- Those events are seen only by the people in your household who can see its calendar, like any event in it, and we do nothing else with them. We share them with nobody, apart from Google Cloud, which runs our servers, and Apple's and Google's notification services when your morning digest arrives as a notification. They are never given to our AI features, to AI assistants you connect to NestPrep, or to the calendar feed you can share.
- Disconnecting deletes the credential and the copied events straight away, and for a Google calendar it also cancels our access with Google. Leaving a household or deleting your account does the same for every calendar you connected.
- If you add a calendar by pasting a **calendar link**, we keep that link on our servers only, because the link itself is the key to that calendar.
- If you **share the household calendar as a feed**, we create a secret link and keep only a scrambled form (a hash) of it. Anybody with the link can read the household's events, so treat it like a key; you can reset it at any time.
- A view of how the week's work was shared between the household's adults is worked out on your phone from what the app already shows you. It is not stored.

### Documents and personal vaults

Households can keep documents such as a lease, insurance papers and school letters in shared folders, and each person has a **private vault** for identity documents, passports, birth certificates and medical aid cards.

- The files themselves, and for each one its name, type, size, tags, an expiry date if you set one, and who uploaded it and when.
- A **view log** for every vault: every time a vault document is opened, we record which document, who opened it and when, before the file can be read. The owner and the family can read that log, and nobody can edit it.
- If you **share a single document by link**, we keep the link's settings, when it expires and how often it was opened. We store only a scrambled form of the link and of any PIN you set.
- If you choose to **keep a document on your phone** for use without a connection, that copy is encrypted on your phone with a key held in your phone's secure storage. It is deleted when you sign out or lose access to the document. We cannot read it.

Identity documents are among the most sensitive things you can give us. The vault only opens after your phone's own screen lock (fingerprint, face or passcode), a helper or carer sees nothing in a vault unless they are granted it, and every open is logged.

### Where people are (location sharing)

Location sharing is off unless you turn it on, and only you can turn it on for yourself. Nobody, not even a household admin, can start, extend or resume sharing your location. A household admin can only remove a position, never create one.

There are two ways to share:

- **For a while.** You choose 15 minutes, one hour or four hours. For that time your phone sends its position while the app is open, and the window ends on its own.
- **All the time** (Premium). Your phone keeps sending its position in the background, including when NestPrep is closed and after it restarts, until you pause it. Before your phone asks for permission, NestPrep shows you what this means; your phone then asks you to allow location "all the time". While it is on, an Android phone shows a notification saying so, and you can pause it in one tap. It also stops by itself if your household's Premium ends, you sign out, or you leave the household.

When you share, we collect:

- **Your current position**, how accurate it is, when it was sent, and when the share ends.
- **Your phone's battery level** and whether it is charging, and **whether you seem to be still, on foot, cycling or driving**, worked out by your phone from its motion sensors, so it can switch location off while you are still and save battery.
- **Where you have been** (Premium, while sharing all the time): the points your phone recorded during the day, kept for **30 days** and then deleted automatically. You can delete your own history at any time.
- **Arrivals and departures** (Premium): when your phone reaches or leaves one of your household's places, such as home or school. The household chooses its places and whether it hears about arrivals, departures or both. A notification to a parent names who arrived and where, and can show on that parent's lock screen. Anybody else who can see the map is told on their lock screen only that somebody arrived; the name and the place are inside the app.

**Children.** A child's phone signed in as their profile (a kid device) can share their location in the same way. A parent may set this up while holding the child's phone; the switch stays on the child's phone, and nobody can turn it on from another device. Children's location is personal information of a child under POPIA, and we process it with the consent of a parent or guardian (section 4).

**Who sees it.** Family members (admins and parents) see the household map. Children in the household see it too, unless a parent turns it off for them. Helpers and carers see it only if a parent grants them the location area; a new helper or carer does not see it. A helper who joined before NestPrep let parents choose area by area keeps seeing everything, the map included, until a parent chooses for them. Anybody who can see the map can see everybody's history in the household.

**What else is involved.** The map is drawn by Google Maps on your phone, which sends Google the area of the map being shown. Street names for a position are worked out by your phone's own system service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android). We never use location for advertising, never sell it, and never share it outside your household except with the service providers named in section 6. A shop you search through NestPrep is sent only a rough position, as described under Shops you link below.

### The nanny hub and home care

- Child cards for carers, an emergency sheet with contacts, a house guide with photos, house rules and shift checklists.
- **Who may collect each child**: their name, relationship, photo and a note about their identification, and the weekly school run.
- Shifts: when a carer started and ended one, what they logged (meals, naps, nappies, moods, incidents and medicine given), photo updates they sent, and the summary written for the parents at the end.
- Home care: cleaning jobs with photos of the spot before and after, the room, and the products and steps to use.

Photos taken in the hub and in home care are compressed and have their location and camera details removed before they are stored.

### Subscriptions

Premium is bought through the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store. **We never see your card or bank details**; the store does. We receive from the store a purchase record (for Google Play a purchase token, for Apple a transaction identifier), which product was bought, when it renews or ends, and whether it is still active. We store that against the household it gives premium to and the account that linked it, and we check it with the store to confirm it is genuine.

### Notifications

If you allow notifications, your phone gives us a push token, a random address for your device that Apple and Google use to deliver notifications. We keep it with your account so we can send your morning digest and reminders, and delete it when you sign out or delete your account.

### AI features

Some features use artificial intelligence, for example reading a school letter you photograph into proposed calendar events that you then check and confirm. These features:

- Are called only from our servers, never directly from your phone.
- Use **Google's Gemini models on Vertex AI** to read photos, documents and web pages and to write suggestions, in the **European Union** (the Netherlands). Pictures of a lunch box are drawn on Google's global infrastructure from a description that names only foods.
- Use **TypeSafe's Jev classification model**, in the **United States**, to choose between a supermarket's products: which one matches an item on your grocery list, whether one suits a lunch box, and how many lunch boxes a pack fills. It is sent only product names, brands and prices, the grocery item as your household wrote it, and the packing preferences you picked, never anything about a person.
- Send only what the task needs. We replace names with placeholders where we can (a child is "child-1"), and we never send allergies, medication, member identifiers or contact details. The photo or document you choose to have read is sent because it has to be; the app tells you so before you pick it, and **we do not keep it** afterwards. The same goes for the text of a web page you ask us to turn into a recipe, and the pictures of a PDF's pages: nothing about your family goes with them. The one exception is an avatar creation, which the family keeps as a keepsake (below).
- Are not used to train anybody's models: Google does not train on what we send, under Google Cloud's data processing terms, and TypeSafe's terms do not allow it to train or fine-tune models on what we send.
- Keep a count per household of how many AI requests were made each month, with no content, so we can apply fair-use limits.

AI suggestions are proposals: nothing is added to your household until you confirm it.

**Google user data is never used for AI.** Information we receive from Google APIs, such as the events of a connected Google Calendar, is never sent to an AI model or service, raw, aggregated or derived, and is never used to develop, improve or train generalised or foundational AI or machine-learning models, ours or anybody else's.

### AI avatar creations

A household can ask our AI to make an avatar: a child's drawing turned into a toy, a Build-a-Buddy put together from words a child taps, a pet turned into a toy twin, or a Nest portrait of the whole household together. This is what happens to what you give it.

- **Everything is checked first, in the European Union.** A drawing, a toy or a pet photo you choose is first looked at by Google's Gemini on Vertex AI in the Netherlands, which says what kind of picture it is. Words typed into a Build-a-Buddy are checked there too.
- **A drawing of a person is never sent on.** If a drawing shows a person, such as a child's picture of themselves, only a few words about its colours and the things in it go to the picture model, and the new picture is drawn from those words alone.
- **A photo of a person is refused**, goes no further, and is deleted.
- **Drawings of creatures and objects, and photos of toys and pets, are sent to Google's Gemini image model**, which draws the new picture. That model runs on Google's global infrastructure, because Google does not offer it to us in a single region, so this picture may be processed outside South Africa and the European Union, under Google Cloud's data processing terms. No names or profile details go with it.
- **A Nest portrait** is drawn from each member's NestPrep character or AI creation. A photo avatar is never used; that member appears as a NestPrep character instead.
- **Photo avatars are never sent to any AI.**
- **We keep what was made, and what it was made from.** Unlike our other AI features, a creation is kept in the household as a keepsake: the finished picture beside the original drawing or photo, or for a Build-a-Buddy the words chosen. Everybody in the household can see the keepsakes, and they stay until the family deletes them. If a request is refused or does not work, the drawing or photo you gave it is deleted. A Nest portrait is also added to the household's photo album for its NestPrep Home frames, where the family can delete it like any other photo.
- **Limits.** Making a creation is part of Premium: a Premium household can make 12 a month. We count them per household, with no content, like the other AI requests.

A new creation becomes a member's avatar only when they choose to use it.

### Shops you link: Checkers, Pick n Pay and Woolworths

NestPrep can find the things on your grocery list at Checkers Sixty60, Pick n Pay and Woolworths, and put them in **your own cart** at that shop. NestPrep never checks out and never pays; you do that in the shop's own app. NestPrep is not affiliated with these shops. They are not our service providers, and what they receive is handled under their own privacy policies and terms.

- **Looking for products.** Your phone asks the shop directly, without an account. It sends the words of the grocery item or lunch idea, and a rough position so the shop can choose a nearby store and its prices: the centre of the area you chose, or your phone's own position only if you have already allowed NestPrep to use location. Pick n Pay is also sent that area's town and postal code. Woolworths' search is run by its search provider, Constructor.io, which is also sent a random identifier for the search. We do not keep the position. We keep only the product you pick, on the grocery item. When a NestPrep Home frame looks for a product, our servers ask the shop instead of a phone.
- **Linking your Checkers account.** The mobile number and the one-time SMS code you type are passed through our servers to Checkers. We keep a masked form of the number and the session Checkers gives us, encrypted, for the hour it lasts.
- **Linking your Pick n Pay account.** You sign in on Pick n Pay's own page inside the app, so we never see your password. We keep the permission Pick n Pay gives us, encrypted, on our servers.
- **Linking your Woolworths account.** You type your Woolworths email address and password in NestPrep, and your phone sends them straight to Woolworths' own sign-in service, which runs on Amazon Web Services. They never reach our servers and we never store them. We keep a masked form of the email address and the permission Woolworths gives us, encrypted, on our servers.
- **Filling your cart.** Only when you tap to add to your cart, our servers send the shop the products and quantities you picked, using that link.

Linking a shop is your choice and only links your own account; nobody else in your household can use it. You can unlink a shop at any time under Account, then Your shops, which deletes what we hold for it. Deleting your account does the same.

### AI assistants you connect (Claude and ChatGPT)

An admin or parent can connect **Claude** (made by Anthropic) or **ChatGPT** (made by OpenAI) to one household, and then ask it to read and change the household's calendar and grocery list. No assistant is connected unless you connect it, on a NestPrep page that shows what it will be allowed to do.

- **What a connected assistant can reach**, within what you allowed: the household's name and time zone, its members' first names and whether each is an adult or a child, the events in the household calendar, and the grocery list. It can add, change and delete events, and add, tick, change and remove grocery items.
- **What it can never reach**: allergies, medication, birthdays, documents and vaults, location, the nanny hub, home care, and events copied from a connected Google or Outlook calendar.
- **Where that goes.** What the assistant reads is sent to Anthropic or OpenAI, in the United States, and is handled under that company's own terms and privacy policy and the settings of your account with them. They are not our service providers.
- **Photos stay with the assistant.** If you give an assistant a photo, such as an invitation, it reads the photo itself. NestPrep never receives it.
- **What we keep**: the connection (who made it, for which household, which assistant, what it may do, and when it was made and last used) and, for 90 days, a record of each change the assistant made. An event or grocery item an assistant added is marked as added through it. The keys the assistant uses are stored only in scrambled form.

You can see and disconnect every assistant under More, then Connected assistants, and family members can switch assistants off for the whole household. Disconnecting stops access straight away. Leaving the household stops your connections working, and deleting your account removes them.

### Product numbers, crash reports and security checks

- **Product numbers.** To know whether NestPrep is working we count weekly active households, lunch plans created per week and how many invites are sent. These are counted on our servers from random identifiers. We do **not** use an advertising or analytics SDK, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and the weekly totals contain no names or identifiers.
- **Crash reports.** When the app crashes it sends a report to Google's Firebase Crashlytics: the technical details of the crash, your device model and operating system version, the app version, and a random member identifier so we can tell how many people a crash affects. We do not put names, email addresses or anything you typed into crash reports.
- **App integrity checks.** We use Firebase App Check, which asks Google Play Integrity or Apple App Attest to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of our app on a genuine device. This protects your household's data from scripts pretending to be the app.

### Our website

Our website sets **no cookies**, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other companies. If you ask us to delete an account through the website, we keep the email address and message you send us only until we have dealt with the request.

## 4. Why we are allowed to use it

POPIA allows us to process personal information only when there is a lawful reason. Ours are:

- **To provide the service you signed up for** (performance of our agreement with you): your account, your household, and everything the app does for it.
- **Your consent**, and for children, the consent of a parent or guardian: children's information, health information such as allergies and medication, live location, connecting outside calendars, linking a shop account, connecting an AI assistant, notifications, and AI features, avatar creations included. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning the feature off, deleting the information, or deleting your account. Withdrawing does not make earlier processing unlawful.
- **Our legitimate interests**, balanced against yours: keeping the service secure (App Check, rate limits, logs without personal details), fixing crashes, and counting the product numbers described above without identifying anybody.
- **Legal obligations**: keeping records the law requires, such as records of purchases, and responding to lawful requests.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 5. Children's information and health information

NestPrep is used by adults to organise family life, and it holds information about children because that is what school lunches, chores and childcare are about.

- **Only an adult can create an account.** You must be 18 or older to sign up. Children do not sign up themselves; a child uses NestPrep only on a kid device a parent has signed in, or as a profile an adult manages.
- **A parent or guardian consents for the child.** POPIA allows a child's personal information to be processed with the consent of a competent person, meaning a parent or legal guardian. When an adult adds a child's profile or marks a profile as a child, the app asks them to confirm that they are the child's parent or guardian, or are acting with that person's authority, and that they consent. We record who confirmed it, when, and against which version of this policy.
- **Health information is kept for the child's safety.** Allergies and food rules are used to keep unsafe food out of lunch plans and to show carers what a child must avoid. Medication is visible only to the people the household's parents allow.
- **A kid device holds no account details.** It has no email address or password, and it sees only what the child's parents allow it to see.
- **A child's avatar is their information too**, and the same consent covers it: a photo avatar, an AI creation and the drawing it was made from. A child on a kid device can change their own avatar, a photo included, spend their stars on accessories and ask for an AI creation. A photo of a person is never made into one, and a drawing of a person never reaches the picture model (section 3).
- A parent can view, correct or delete any of their child's information at any time, and can sign out every kid device.

## 6. Who can see your information

- **Your household.** Everybody in the household, helpers, carers and children included, sees each member's avatar, and so do the household's NestPrep Home frames. Family members (admins and parents) see the household's information. Helpers, carers and kids see only the areas the parents grant them. Your private vault is visible to you and your household's parents, and to anyone else only if you or they grant it.
- **People you share with.** If you share a single document by link or share the calendar feed, anybody who has that link can see what it points to until it expires or you reset it.
- **Our service providers**, who process information on our behalf and under contract. They are listed below.
- **Authorities**, only where the law requires it and after we have checked that the request is lawful.

Our service providers are:

- **Google** (Google Cloud and Firebase): our database, file storage, servers, sign-in, crash reports, app integrity checks and push notifications; Vertex AI and Gemini's image model for the AI features; and Google Maps, which draws the family map.
- **TypeSafe**: the Jev classification model that chooses between supermarket products for the AI features.
- **Apple and Google Play**: in-app purchases, subscription management, app integrity checks and push notification delivery.
- **Google and Microsoft**, only if you connect your Google or Outlook calendar, to read your events.

Some companies receive information only because you chose to use them through NestPrep. They are not our service providers, and they handle what they receive under their own terms and privacy policies:

- **Shoprite Checkers, Pick n Pay and Woolworths**, when NestPrep looks for products at that shop, and when you link your own account there or add to your cart. Woolworths uses Constructor.io for its product search and Amazon Web Services for its sign-in.
- **Anthropic and OpenAI**, only if you connect Claude or ChatGPT to a household.

Nobody at NestPrep looks at your household's content unless you ask us to help with something and give us permission, or the law requires it.

## 7. Where your information is kept, and transfers outside South Africa

Our database, file storage and servers run in **Google Cloud's Johannesburg region (africa-south1), in South Africa**.

Some processing happens outside South Africa:

- AI features run in the **European Union** (Vertex AI in the Netherlands), apart from lunch-box pictures, drawn on Google's global infrastructure, and product choices, made by TypeSafe in the **United States**. Neither is sent anything about a person.
- **Avatar creations** are checked in the European Union first. The picture is then drawn by Google's Gemini image model on Google's global infrastructure, so it may be processed outside South Africa and the European Union. That model is sent only a drawing of a creature or an object, a photo of a toy or a pet, NestPrep characters and earlier creations, or a few words, never a photo of a person or a drawing of one.
- Sign-in, crash reports, app integrity checks and push notifications are run by Google, Apple and Microsoft on their global infrastructure, which may include the United States and Europe.
- If you connect an **AI assistant**, what it reads from your household is sent to Anthropic or OpenAI in the **United States**.
- If you use **Woolworths** through NestPrep, its product search and sign-in are run by its own providers, which may process them outside South Africa.

POPIA (section 72) allows these transfers because the recipients are bound by law or by binding agreements that give protection substantially similar to POPIA's, including the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation and Google Cloud's data processing terms, and because they are necessary to provide the service you asked for. What goes to a shop you use or an assistant you connect is sent at your request and with your consent, each time you use it. Where an avatar picture is drawn outside South Africa and the European Union, Google Cloud's data processing terms bind Google to that protection wherever it processes it, and each creation is one your household chose to make.

## 8. How we keep it safe

- Everything is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google Cloud.
- Every read and write is checked against your household's roles and grants on our servers, not only in the app, and each of those checks is tested.
- Vault documents open only behind your phone's own lock, every opening is logged by the server first, and a helper sees no identity document unless granted it.
- Credentials for connected calendars and linked shops, purchase records and share-link secrets are held only on our servers, and links, PINs and the keys of connected assistants are stored scrambled.
- Our logs record what happened, not who you are: no names, email addresses, tokens or content.
- App Check and rate limits protect us from automated abuse.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires, as soon as reasonably possible.

## 9. How long we keep it

- **While your account exists**, we keep your account and the information in your households so the app can work.
- **Things you delete** are removed from the live service straight away. A shared position is deleted when you stop sharing or the window ends; a position from a phone that stopped sharing all the time without being paused is treated as not sharing after a day and deleted automatically. Location history and arrivals are deleted automatically after 30 days, or straight away when you delete them. When you leave a household or are removed from it, your position, your history and the arrival notifications that name you are deleted from that household. A photo avatar is deleted when it is changed or removed. Deleting an avatar keepsake deletes both the creation and its original. When a member is removed from a household, or deletes their account, their avatar photos, creations and keepsakes are deleted with them. If you only leave a household, your profile stays there with its avatar until the family removes it. A Nest portrait in the household's photo album belongs to the household and stays until the family deletes it. Invite and kid pairing codes expire and are removed. Document share links stop working when they expire and are then cleared. What we hold for a linked shop is deleted when you unlink it, and a Checkers session after its hour. - **Weekly product numbers** are totals with no identifiers and are kept.

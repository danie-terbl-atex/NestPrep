Premium and billing

  • Premium is bought through the App Store or Google Play, and they take the payment. You can see, change or cancel your plan in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
  • Bought Premium but the app still shows the free plan? On the Premium screen in the app, choose Restore purchases. If that does not help, email us.
  • Refunds are handled by the store you bought from.

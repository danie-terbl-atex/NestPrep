NestPrep is built around a household: a group of people who share a calendar, lists, lunch plans and documents.

  • The person who creates a household is its first admin. Admins invite people, choose each person's role (admin, parent, kid, helper or carer) and decide what each helper, carer and kid may see and change.
  • You are responsible for what you put into a household about other people. When you add another person's details, such as a child's allergies, a grandparent's medication, a nanny's contact number or a copy of somebody's ID, you confirm that you are entitled to do so: that the information is theirs to share and they have agreed, or that you are their parent or guardian or otherwise have the authority to act for them.
  • Admins decide who sees what. Before you give a helper or carer access to an area, make sure you are comfortable with them seeing everything in it. You can change or remove access at any time.
  • A household keeps its shared records, such as events, lists and documents, when a member leaves. What happens to a household when its last adult deletes their account is explained in our Privacy Policy.

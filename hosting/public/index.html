School lunches, sorted before Sunday night.

NestPrep plans a week of school lunches for each child, keeping to their allergies and their school's rules, and keeps the rest of the family's week in the same place.

One household, one app

  • Lunch boxes, planned per child

    A week of lunches on one grid, built from your own items. Anything a child cannot have stays out, and NestPrep learns what actually comes home uneaten.

  • Family profiles

    Likes, dislikes, allergies, school and sizes for everybody, so lunches, carers and the grocery list all start from the same facts.

  • The family week

    Everybody's events in their own colour, added in plain words or snapped from a school letter, with your Google and Outlook calendars alongside.

  • A grocery list that fills itself

    What the week's meals and lunches need lands on the list. Anybody can tick it off in the shop.

  • To-dos and chores

    Routines that repeat, jobs with a name on them, and chores that earn children stars they spend on rewards you choose.

  • The papers you keep needing

    Shared folders for the household, and a private vault for each person's IDs and passports that opens behind your phone's own lock.

  • A hub for the nanny

    Child cards, the emergency sheet, house rules and shift checklists, with a handover back to you when the shift ends.

  • Home care a helper can follow

    Turn a photo of a spot into a cleaning job with the right products, the steps and the safety warnings.

Built to hold children's information carefully

  • Only adults create accounts. Children use a device a parent signs in.
  • Parents decide what a helper, carer or child can see, and the server enforces it, not just the app.
  • Identity documents open behind your phone's lock, and every opening is logged.
  • Your household's data is stored in South Africa. We do not sell it, and we do not advertise.
  • Download everything we hold about you, or delete your account, from inside the app.

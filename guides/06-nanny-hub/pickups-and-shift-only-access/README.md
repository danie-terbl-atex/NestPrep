# Pickups, booked shifts and house codes

**Who it's for:** a parent. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone, then the nanny's phone inside and outside a shift.

**Before filming:** the `nannyPickups` and `nannyShiftOnly` switches on. Shift-only access is admin only.

## Steps: parent
1. Nanny hub → **School run and pickups** → **Who may collect** → **Add a person**: name, "Who they are to the family", "How to be sure it is them", "Who may they collect?".
2. Set **The school-run week**, then **Add a change for a day** for exceptions.
3. **Booked shifts** → switch on **Only during booked shifts** → **Book a shift** (day, start, end) → **Book it**.
4. **House codes** → **Add a code** (what it's for, the code) → **Save**.

## What the nanny sees
- **At the door:** Door check → "Who is at the door?". If the person isn't on the list: "Not on this list? Do not release {child}."
- **House codes:** they show only during her shift. Otherwise: "Codes open during your shift".
- **Outside a booked shift:** the whole app is "See you at your next shift", with her booked shifts and **Check again**. Her access opens 15 minutes before a shift and closes 15 minutes after.

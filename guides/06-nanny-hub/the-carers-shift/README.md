# The carer's shift (what the nanny sees)

**Who it's for:** parents who want to see the nanny's side, and nannies themselves. **Devices on screen:** the nanny's phone, then the parent's phone as the pushes arrive.

## Steps: nanny's phone
1. **More → Around the house → Nanny hub → Start my shift** opens **On shift**.
2. **What happened?** → Meal, Nap, Nappy, Mood, Incident, Medicine or Note → **Log it**.
3. **Checklist** panel: tick items ("N of M done").
4. **Send the parents a photo** → **Open the camera** → a few words → **Send to the parents**.
5. Child cards, **Emergency** and **House rules** are always one tap away.
6. **End shift** → a last word for the parents → **End shift** opens the **Handover**: her note, the checklists, what happened, and photos.

## Steps: parent's phone
1. A push arrives: "A photo from the shift".
2. Another push: "The shift handover is ready" → open the handover.
3. The next morning digest has a nanny hub section ("Handover from {name}").

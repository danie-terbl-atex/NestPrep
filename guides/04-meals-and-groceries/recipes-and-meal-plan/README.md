# Recipes and the meal plan

**Who it's for:** a parent. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone.

**Before filming:** Premium, for Add to grocer.

## Steps
1. **Food → Meals → Recipes.** Search ("Bobotie, chicken, pap…") or filter by meal (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner…) and tags (Braai, Quick, Kid-friendly).
2. Open a recipe. It shows time, effort and serves, allergens, ingredients to tick, and the method.
3. Adjust "Cooking for N people" with **Cook for one more** / **Cook for one fewer**.
4. **Add to this week** → pick the week, day and meal → "{dish} is on Tuesday's dinner".
5. **This week:** tap a slot → "What are we eating?" → **Pick a NestPrep recipe**, type your own, or choose from "Meals you have made before".
6. **Copy last week** fills the week from the one before.
7. **Add to grocer** (Premium) on a planned recipe: the "Shop for this recipe" card finds the ingredients at your shop. Untick or **Change** any → **Add N to the grocery list** or **Add to my {shop} cart**.
8. You can also send a recipe to the kitchen frame: [cook-along-on-the-frame](../../10-nestprep-home-frame/cook-along-on-the-frame/).

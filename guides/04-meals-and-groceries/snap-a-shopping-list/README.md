# Snap a shopping list

**Who it's for:** anyone with a list on paper or the fridge. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone photographing a handwritten list.

**Before filming:** Premium, and the `snapShoppingList` switch on.

## Steps
1. **Lists → Groceries** → the snap button beside the add field.
2. **Take a photo** or **Choose a photo** → "Reading your list…" → "We read N items".
3. Untick or fix any line.
4. **Add N to the list**, or **Find at {shop}** to match the items to products.
5. **Back to the list**, or **Snap another list**.

# Fill your Checkers (or Pick n Pay) cart

**Who it's for:** a parent. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone, then the Checkers Sixty60 app showing the filled cart.

**Before filming:** a linked shop ([link-your-shops](../link-your-shops/)).

## Steps
1. **Lists → Groceries.** Tap the shop logo in the add field to turn on **Match as you type**. Matching Checkers products show as you type, or tap **Just add "…"**.
2. For an item already on the list, tap the logo beside it (**Find at Checkers**).
3. "At Checkers: {item}" shows products marked Best match (chosen by nestprep), Deal and Out of stock → **Pick**.
4. The product now sits under the item, with **Change product** and **Remove product**.
5. The quantity counts packs ("3 packs"); anything else counts as one pack.
6. The bar reads "N items have a Checkers product" → **Add N to Checkers**.
7. Results: "N items added / not added", with reasons (sold by weight, out of stock), and "Your cart now has … · R…".
8. Open **Checkers Sixty60** to finish and pay. "NestPrep never checks out for you."
9. Pick n Pay works the same way and finishes in the Pick n Pay app or website. Woolworths finishes in Woolies Dash.

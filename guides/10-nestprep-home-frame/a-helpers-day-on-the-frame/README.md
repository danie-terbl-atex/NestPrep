# A helper's day on the frame

**Who it's for:** a parent with a helper. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone, then the frame with the helper ticking jobs.

**Before filming:** a helper in the household ([add-a-helper](../../02-family-and-people/add-a-helper/)).

## Steps
1. Phone: **More → Photo frames** → the frame → **Frame settings**.
2. Switch on **A helper's day** → **Whose day?** → pick her → **Save**.
3. Frame: **To do** now has a list called "{Name}'s day", with her chores, her jobs from today and any overdue, and today's room routines.
4. She ticks chores and routine steps on the wall. She doesn't need to sign in on the frame.
5. Cleaning jobs with steps and an after photo say "Steps and the hand-in photo happen in NestPrep", so those are done on her phone ([the-helpers-day](../../07-home-care/the-helpers-day/)).

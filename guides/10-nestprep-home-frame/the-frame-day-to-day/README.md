# The frame day to day

**Who it's for:** the whole family. **Devices on screen:** the frame on the kitchen wall.

## Steps
1. The top bar shows the clock, date, household name and weather.
2. The side rail has Calendar, Chores, To do, Lunch boxes, Meals, Groceries and Frame settings.
3. **Calendar:** Schedule, Day, Family, Week and Month views. Tap an event for its card.
4. **Lunch boxes:** "{Name}'s box", with Main, Fruit, Veg, Snack and Treat, plus a picture of the box (an AI photo with Premium).
5. **Meals today:** breakfast, lunch and dinner, with **Next up**.
6. **Screensaver:**
   1. In the app: Photo frames → **Screensaver photos** → **Add photos** (up to 100).
   2. In the frame's settings: **Screensaver** → set the idle minutes and how long each photo shows.
7. When idle, the frame plays the family photos with the clock. Touch it to wake it.

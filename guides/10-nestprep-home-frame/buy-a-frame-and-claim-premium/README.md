# Buy a frame and claim your Premium

**Who it's for:** a family buying the frame. **Devices on screen:** the store on a laptop or phone, the confirmation email, then the app.

## Steps
1. On the store, open **Family calendar frame** → choose **10-inch** (2 months of Premium) or **15-inch** (3 months) → choose a colour (Black, White, Wood).
2. **Pre-order now** (or **Add to basket**) → **Your basket** (optional discount code) → **Continue to delivery**.
3. **Delivery details** → **Review order** → **Check and pay** → **Pay R… securely**.
4. "Paid. Your frame is on its way soon." An email follows: "…your free nestprep Premium is ready".
5. In the app, signed in with **the same email**, a **Premium waiting for you** sheet opens → **Add to [household]**.
6. If you ordered with another email: **Ordered with a different email?** → **Send code** → enter the 6-digit code → **Confirm**.
7. Giving the frame as a gift: open the email's gift link → **Send it to a different email**.

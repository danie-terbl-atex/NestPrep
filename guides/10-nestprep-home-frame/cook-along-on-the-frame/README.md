# Cook along on the frame

**Who it's for:** whoever is cooking. **Devices on screen:** the phone, then the frame in the kitchen.

**Before filming:** **Open recipes and pages shared from phones** switched on for the frame.

## Steps
1. Phone: open a recipe → **Open on frame** → **Show on frame**, or pick which frame → "Opening on Kitchen frame".
2. Frame: **Get ready** shows the ingredients to tick → **Start cooking**.
3. **Step 3 of 7**, with **Back** and **Next**. Tap a time in a step to start a timer.
4. "Time's up — Step 6", with **Stop** and **+3 min**.
5. **Finish** → "Enjoy your …" → **Done**.
6. **Web pages:** in the phone's browser, tap Share → **NestPrep** → **Show on frame**. The frame opens that one page, "Sent by {name}", with **Close**.

## Not live yet / check first
- Photos can't be shared to the frame this way; they reach it through the screensaver album.
- Without a frame, **Open on frame** shows a teaser instead: "Cook along on the kitchen wall".

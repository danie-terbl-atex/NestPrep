# Manage your frames

**Who it's for:** a parent. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone and the frame.

## Steps
1. **More → Photo frames** lists each frame, with "Seen 2 min ago" or **Waiting to hear from it**.
2. Tap a frame → **Frame settings**: the name, what it shows, groceries, the screensaver → **Save**.
3. **Unlink from the phone:** **Unlink frame** → **Unlink frame** again (or **Keep it**). The frame goes back to its code.
4. **Unlink on the frame:** rail → Frame settings → **Hold to unlink this frame**.
5. **Another frame:** **Link a frame** again. Each frame has its own panels, up to 5 per household, and they all share one screensaver album.

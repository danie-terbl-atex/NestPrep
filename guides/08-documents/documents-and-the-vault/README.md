# Documents and the vault

**Who it's for:** Admins and Parents. Documents are hidden from every other role. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone.

## Steps
1. **Shared documents** → **Add a folder** (admins), e.g. "School".
2. Open the folder → **Add a document** → **Scan** (the front and back are saved as one PDF) or **Choose a file** → **Add to the folder**.
3. **Edit details** → **Add tag** and **Add an expiry date**. Reminders come 90, 30 and 7 days before, and on the day.
4. **Find a document**, filtered by Everyone, Household or Expiring soon.
5. **Personal vaults** → **Unlock** with face, fingerprint or PIN → **Save to the vault**. "Who opened what" shows every view. **Lock the vaults** locks them again.
6. **Share a link** → "How long it works", optional **Ask for a PIN** → **Make the link** → **Copy**. **Stop sharing** ends it.
7. **Keep on this phone** saves an offline copy.

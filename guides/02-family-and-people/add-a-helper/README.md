# Add a helper (domestic worker)

**Who it's for:** a parent bringing in a cleaner or domestic worker. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone, then the helper's phone.

## Steps
1. **More → People → Manage people → Invite someone → Helper**.
2. Type her name → **Create invite** → **Share invite**.
3. Optional: change **What they can see** → **Save**.
4. The helper joins with **I have a code**, the same way as any invite.
5. Set her language under Home care → **Languages**, then give her jobs: [set-up-cleaning-jobs](../../07-home-care/set-up-cleaning-jobs/).

## What the helper sees (default)
- **Home care** shows "Your cleaning jobs": only the jobs given to her, in her chosen language, with read aloud.
- She can **change** the grocery list, so she can add what's running out.
- She can **see** the calendar and meal plan. Food opens on Meals, because lunch boxes are hidden from her.
- She sees **only her own** to-dos.
- Hidden from her: lunch boxes, family profiles, medication, the nanny hub, documents and Where we are.
- Her day can also go on the wall: [a-helpers-day-on-the-frame](../../10-nestprep-home-frame/a-helpers-day-on-the-frame/).
- Following a job on her phone: [the-helpers-day](../../07-home-care/the-helpers-day/).

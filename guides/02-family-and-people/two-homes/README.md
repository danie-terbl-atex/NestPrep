# Two homes (a child in two households)

**Who it's for:** co-parents who live apart. **Devices on screen:** a parent's phone in each home.

**Before filming:** the `coParenting` switch must be on, and both people must be Admins or Parents.

## Steps
1. Home A: **More → Two homes → Link with another home**.
2. Answer **Which child?**, name **Your home** and pick its colour, set **The schedule**, then **Make a code** → **Share the code**.
3. Home B: More → Two homes → **I have a code** → **Check the code** → **Accept and link**, or **Add [child] as a new kid**.
4. Home A confirms with **Yes, link our homes**.
5. From then on: **Ask for a swap**, **Suggest a new schedule**, Handovers, and **End the link**.
6. The other home's reminders are under notification settings ("The other home").

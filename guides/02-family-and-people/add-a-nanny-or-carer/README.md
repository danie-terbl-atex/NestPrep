# Add a nanny or carer

**Who it's for:** a parent deciding what a nanny will see. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone, then the nanny's phone.

## Steps
1. **More → People → Manage people → Invite someone → Nanny or carer**.
2. Type her name → **Create invite** → **Share invite**.
3. Optional: under **What they can see**, set each area to Hidden, Only theirs, Can see or Can change → **Save**. **Suggested for the role** resets it.
4. The nanny installs nestprep → **I have a code** → enters the code → creates her account → **Join [household]**.
5. Set up her hub before her first shift: [set-up-the-nanny-hub](../../06-nanny-hub/set-up-the-nanny-hub/).

## What the nanny sees (default)
- The same five tabs as a parent, showing only what she's allowed.
- **More** shows **Around the house** first, with the **Nanny hub**: Start my shift, child cards, the emergency sheet, house rules.
- She can **see** the calendar, meal plan, lunch boxes, family profiles and medication. She can **change** the nanny hub. She sees **only her own** to-dos.
- Hidden from her: documents, home care and Where we are.
- If shift-only access is on, outside her shifts she sees only "See you at your next shift". See [pickups-and-shift-only-access](../../06-nanny-hub/pickups-and-shift-only-access/).
- What a shift looks like on her phone: [the-carers-shift](../../06-nanny-hub/the-carers-shift/).

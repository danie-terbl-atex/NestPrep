# Invite a co-parent or family member

**Who it's for:** the main parent (Admin). **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone, then the invitee's phone.

## Steps
1. **More → People → Manage people → Invite someone**.
2. Pick who they are: **Partner or co-parent** (joins as Admin) or **Grandparent** (joins as Parent).
3. On **Who are you inviting?**, type their name, check **They are** (the role) → **Create invite**.
4. On **Code for [name]**, tap **Share invite** (WhatsApp, SMS) or **Copy code**. The code works once and lasts seven days.
5. The invitee installs nestprep → **I have a code** → types the 8 characters → signs in or creates an account → agrees to the terms → **Join [household]**.
6. Back on the parent's phone, they now appear under People.

## What they see
- **Admin:** everything, including managing people and billing.
- **Parent:** everything except managing people, invites and access.

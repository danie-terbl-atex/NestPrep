# Set up cleaning jobs

**Who it's for:** a parent. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone.

## Steps
1. **More → Around the house → Home care**.
2. **Rooms** → **Add the usual rooms** or **Add a room**.
3. **Products** → **Add a product**: its name, what kind of product it is, where it's kept.
4. **New job** → **Take a photo** → **Circle the spot** → **Done**.
5. Fill in "What needs doing", Room, "Who will do it", "Done by" and "Products to use".
6. **Steps** → **Add a step**, or tap a suggestion ("Open a window").
7. **Safety:** it warns about products that must never be mixed, with "Keep away from children / pets".
8. **Assign the job**.
9. Later, **Review the job** shows the before and after photos → **Approve** or **Send it back**.

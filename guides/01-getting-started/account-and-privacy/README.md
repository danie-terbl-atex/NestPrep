# Your account, data and feedback

**Who it's for:** any member. **Devices on screen:** a parent's phone.

## Steps
1. Tap the **Account** button at the top of Today, Calendar or Food. Its menu has:
   - Switch household (only with more than one)
   - **Account and privacy**
   - **Send feedback**
   - **Replay the tour**
   - **Sign out**
2. **Account and privacy** ("Your data, your choices") → **Download my data** → **Prepare my data** → save or share. The link lasts an hour.
3. Under **Shopping**, **Your shops** is covered in [link-your-shops](../../04-meals-and-groceries/link-your-shops/).
4. Under **About NestPrep**: About, Privacy policy, Terms, Licences.
5. **Send feedback** → "What is it about?" → which part of the app (optional) → "Your message" → optional "You may email me about this" → **Send feedback** → "Thank you".
6. **Delete my account:** it shows what happens to each household → type `DELETE` → **Delete my account**.

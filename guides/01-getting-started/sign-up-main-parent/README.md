# Sign up as the main parent

**Who it's for:** the parent setting nestprep up for the family. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone.

## Steps
1. Download nestprep from the App Store or Google Play and open it.
2. On the sign-in screen, tap **New here? → Create an account**. **Continue with Google** works too.
3. On **Create your account**, fill in Your name, Email and Password (8+ characters), then tap **Create account**.
4. **Step 1 of 3 · A few promises:** tick **I am 18 or older** and **I agree to the Terms and the Privacy Policy**, then **Agree and continue**.
5. **Step 2 of 3 · Your household:** tap **Create a household**, type the Household name (e.g. "The Parkers") and **What should we call you?**, then **Create**.
6. **Step 3 of 3 · Your people:** pick who else keeps the house running (Partner or co-parent, Grandparent, Helper, Nanny or carer, A child with a phone), or **Skip for now**.
7. You land on **Today**, and the welcome tour starts. See [welcome-tour](../welcome-tour/).
8. Next steps: [add-a-child](../../02-family-and-people/add-a-child/) and [invite-co-parent-or-family](../../02-family-and-people/invite-co-parent-or-family/).

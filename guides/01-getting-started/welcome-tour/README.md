# The welcome tour

**Who it's for:** anyone new to nestprep. **Devices on screen:** a parent's phone, then a helper's phone for contrast.

## Steps
1. The tour starts by itself the first time you open **Today**. Move through it with **Next**, **Back**, **Skip** and **Done**.
2. Its stops, in order:
   - the tab bar ("Five places, one family")
   - today's lunch
   - the bell
   - Calendar
   - Lunch: the child switcher, a day, Plan the week, Twig and the lunch tools
   - Meals
   - Lists
   - More: People and Family profiles
   - the account button
3. To replay it: Account button → **Replay the tour**.

## What they see
- **Helpers, nannies and carers** get a shorter tour, showing only what their role can use, with look-only wording where they can only look.
- **A child's own device** gets no tour.

# Kid picks their lunch

**Who it's for:** a parent and child together. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone handed to the child, or the child's own tablet.

**Before filming:** the kid picks switch on.

## Steps
1. Parent: Lunch tools → kid picks → **Choose options** (two or three safe items) → **Offer these**, or **Suggest options for the week**.
2. **Let {name} choose now** hands over the phone.
3. Child: "Pick your lunch!" → **Let's choose** → taps their favourites → "All done! Your lunch box is ready."
4. "All done — give the phone back."
5. On the child's own tablet, the same choices show under **My lunch box**.

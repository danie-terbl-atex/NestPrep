# Plan lunch with Twig (and how the Checkers cart works)

**Who it's for:** a parent. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone, then the Checkers Sixty60 app at the end.

**Before filming:**
- Premium, with the `planMyWeek` switch on.
- Each child's allergies and dislikes filled in.
- To show the cart, a Checkers account linked (see [link-your-shops](../../04-meals-and-groceries/link-your-shops/)).

## Steps
1. **Food → Lunch → Plan the week → Let Twig plan it** ("Lunches for every child from what {shop} sells, within your budget").
2. **Brief:** tick the children. Each shows "Kept out: …" and "Likes: …". Point out "The AI never sees allergies."
3. **How you like to pack:**
   - Packing chips: Prefer ready-made, 10 min prep max, Prep the night before, No fridge needed, and more.
   - **Treat days**: No treats / Fridays only / Every day.
4. **Shop:** tap **Checkers** (or Pick n Pay, Woolworths), and use **Change area** if needed. Then **Set a budget** for the week.
5. **Get lunch ideas.** "Walking the lunchbox aisle…" reads Checkers' own lunchbox shelves near you. Remove any idea or **Add your own idea**.
6. **Look for N ideas at Checkers.** Twig checks every product against each child's rules, and shows why one was left out ("contains nuts", "out of stock").
7. **The week:** tap anything to swap it. "The basket" shows whole packs at the till price → **Use this week**.
8. **Done** ("Your week is planned · Planned by Twig"): **Add to my Checkers cart**, or **Add N products to the grocery list**.

## How the Checkers part works (say this on screen)
- Products come from Checkers' real catalogue, near your address.
- **Add to my Checkers cart** puts the packs into **your own** Sixty60 cart. It only adds what the cart is short of, and never lowers anything.
- You then open **Checkers Sixty60** to check out and pay. nestprep never checks out or pays.
- On Woolworths, items with no published contents show "Woolworths doesn't publish what's in this. Check the label."

## Not live yet / check first
- If Twig is switched off, used up for the month, or the phone is offline, you get a products-only week instead.
- Woolworths is built but not live.

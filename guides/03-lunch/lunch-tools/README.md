# Lunch tools

**Who it's for:** a parent. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone, plus a WhatsApp chat for the share card.

All of these are under **Lunch tools**, at the bottom of Food → Lunch.

## Steps
1. **Sunday prep** (Premium): the "Make ahead" and "Have in the house" lists, ticked together across the household ("N of M ready").
2. **Share the week:**
   1. Choose "Whose lunches" and a shape (Story, Post, WhatsApp).
   2. Choose a look and "Names on the card" → **Share image**.
   3. The card never shows allergies, schools or surnames.
3. **Printable planner:** **This week** or **Blank** (A4) → **Print** or **Send PDF**.
4. **Pantry:**
   1. **Add to the pantry**, then "Plan from what we have".
   2. **Mark packed** takes items out of the pantry.
   3. **Add all N to groceries** adds what the week still needs.
5. **Budget** (Premium): **Set a weekly budget**, then the spent meter, "This week's basket", and "Cheaper swaps for {name}" → **Swap**.
6. **AI lunch photo** (Premium): there's no button. Each packed box's picture fades into a photo of those exact foods. It also shows on the frame.

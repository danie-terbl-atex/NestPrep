# Plan the lunch week yourself

**Who it's for:** a parent. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone.

**Before filming:** a child with allergies and school rules in Family profiles.

## Steps
1. **Food → Lunch** ("Lunch? Lovely."). With more than one child, pick a child at the top. Their allergies and school rules show first.
2. Tap a day pill (Mon–Fri) to open that day's box.
3. Tap an empty compartment (Main, Fruit, Veg, Snack, Treat), e.g. **Add a main**. The picker shows "Suggested for {name}", "{name} does not like" and "Not safe for {name}".
4. **Something else** adds a new food: "What is it?", which part of the box, allergen ticks, "Make it ahead on Sunday" → **Add and pack it**.
5. **More for this day → Save as a go-to box** → name it. Next time, use **Pack a go-to box**.
6. **Plan the week → Pick the boxes yourself** opens **Fill the week**.
7. Choose from "Your go-to boxes" or "Ready-made by NestPrep" → **Review the week**, or **Skip — fill it all for me**.
8. **Fill the week** → **Back to the lunch board**.

## Rules to mention
- It only fills empty days, and never days that have passed.
- Treats go in on Fridays only.

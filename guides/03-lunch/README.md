# 03 Lunch

2. [plan-lunch-with-twig](plan-lunch-with-twig/): Twig plans the week from what Checkers, Pick n Pay or Woolworths sells, then fills your cart.
3. [did-they-eat-it](did-they-eat-it/): what came home, and how it shapes the next lunches.
4. [lunch-tools](lunch-tools/): Sunday prep, the share card, the printable planner, pantry, budget.
5. [kid-picks-their-lunch](kid-picks-their-lunch/): the child chooses from safe options.

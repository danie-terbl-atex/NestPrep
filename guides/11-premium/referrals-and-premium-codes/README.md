# Referrals and Premium codes

**Who it's for:** Admins and Parents. **Devices on screen:** two families' phones, and the redeem page in a browser.

**Before filming:** the `referralRewards` and `premiumVouchers` switches on.

## Steps: give a month, get a month
1. **More → Give a month, get a month** → **Share code** or **Copy code**.
2. The new family: "Joined because of another family?" → enter the code → **Use this code**. This only works in their household's first 7 days.
3. Both households get a month once two adults in the new household use the app, or it buys Premium. The cap is 6 months a year.

## Steps: redeem a Premium code
1. Open the redeem page (`/redeem`) → **Redeem a Premium code** → the code and your email → **Redeem**.
2. In the app: **Premium waiting for you** → **Add to [household]**.

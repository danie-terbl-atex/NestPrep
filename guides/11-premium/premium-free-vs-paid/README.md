# Premium: free vs paid

**Who it's for:** a parent deciding whether to upgrade. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone.

## Steps
1. **Free:** the family calendar; shared lists, to-dos and meals; one child's profile.
2. **Premium adds:**
   - every child
   - learning lunches (Did they eat it)
   - Sunday prep, budget mode and AI lunch photos
   - Twig's Plan my week
   - Add to grocer and Snap a shopping list
   - always-on location, places and history
3. **More → Plan & billing → See Premium → Choose a plan** (Monthly or Yearly) → **Start Premium**.
4. The paywall also opens when you tap a locked feature, e.g. adding a second child.
5. **Restore purchases** is on the plan screen and the paywall.
6. Helpers, carers and children see "Premium is bought by a parent…".

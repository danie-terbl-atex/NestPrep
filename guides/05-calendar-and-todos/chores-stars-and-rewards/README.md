# Chores, stars and rewards

**Who it's for:** a parent setting up chores for a child. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone and the child's tablet. Optionally the frame, for the celebration.

**Before filming:** the child signed in on their device ([add-a-child](../../02-family-and-people/add-a-child/)). Two or more kids need Premium.

## Steps: parent
1. **Add a task** → pick a picture sticker ("Try teeth, dog or dishes") → **Stars for doing it** → optional **A grown-up checks it first** → set **For** to the child.
2. **Stars and rewards → Add a reward:** "What is it?", "How many stars?", and a picture.

## Steps: child (My day)
1. **My jobs today** → tap the chore → **Done!**
2. The stars card grows, and shows streaks.
3. **Treats to save for:** "{n} stars more to go" or "You have enough stars!" → **Get it!** → **Yes please!**
4. "What I asked for" shows "A grown-up will sort it".

## Steps: parent again
1. Under **Waiting for you**, tap **Given** or **Not now**. For checked chores, tap **Looks good** or **Send back**.
2. The child sees "Yours!", or "Not this time · stars back".

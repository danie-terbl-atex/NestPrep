# Today and the morning digest

**Who it's for:** everyone. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone at breakfast.

## Steps
1. **Today** opens with "Morning, {name}." It shows only what your role can see:
   - What's on today, and Coming up this week
   - Left to do
   - **In the lunchboxes**: each child's next box (tap it to open Lunch)
   - On the list ("N things to buy")
2. The top bar has the layout switch (**Switch to the card home**), the bell, and the account menu.
3. The first time, a **Your morning digest** prompt appears → **Turn on notifications**.
4. The digest arrives each morning: On today, What to pack (→ **Open lunch**), Chores, and Waiting for you. "On a day with nothing on, we stay quiet."

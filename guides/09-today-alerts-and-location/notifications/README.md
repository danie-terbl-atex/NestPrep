# Notifications

**Who it's for:** any member. **Devices on screen:** the parent's phone.

## Steps
1. The bell on Today opens the **Notifications** inbox → gear → **Notification settings**.
2. **Send me a morning digest**, and "Arrives at" (06:30 by default).
3. Each kind of reminder has its own switch:
   - Documents to renew
   - Shift handovers
   - Chores and rewards
   - Photos from a shift
   - The other home
   - Arrivals and departures
4. **Hold notifications overnight**, from 21:00 until 06:00. Held items arrive in the morning.
5. Children: **Morning digest on their tablet**.
6. **Send me a test** checks it works.

# Where we are (live location)

**Who it's for:** Admins and Parents (children can see it; helpers and carers can't). **Devices on screen:** two parents' phones and the map.

**Before filming:** Premium for always-on sharing, places and history.

## Steps
1. **More → Where we are** → **Share for** 15 minutes, 1 hour or 4 hours (free) → **Stop sharing**.
2. **Always share** (Premium): "Share where you are, all the time" → **Continue** → choose **Allow all the time** in the phone's prompt. **Pause sharing** pauses it.
3. **Places** (Premium) → **Add a place** → **Use where I am now** → name, icon, radius → "Tell us when somebody arrives / leaves" → **Save**.
4. Tap a member → "Where {name} has been", with Today and Yesterday. History is kept 30 days; **Clear my history** clears your own.
5. Each person shows "Here now" or "Last seen", their place or street, battery and distance.
